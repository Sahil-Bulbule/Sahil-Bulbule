I'm a Computer Technology undergraduate at **YCCE Nagpur** (B.Tech, graduating 2028), and I enjoy turning ideas into software that actually works.

I started with **Python** and **web development**, building small applications to understand how real products come together. Over time, my curiosity pulled me toward **Machine Learning and Deep Learning**, and now toward **Generative AI**: LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Agentic AI systems.

Most of my projects so far are practical, end-to-end apps: a trained model, a clean interface, and a clear problem to solve. I'm working on going deeper into LLM-powered applications and learning what it takes to make them reliable.

**My goal:** to grow into an AI-focused software engineer who builds useful, production-minded intelligent systems.
